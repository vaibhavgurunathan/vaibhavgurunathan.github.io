Kudan Internship

This summer, I was an intern at Kudan. Kudan works to build Simultaneous Localization and Mapping (SLAM) systems. I've signed an NDA here so I cannnot reveal a lot. Most of my internship worked on setting up robots to use Kudan’s SLAM technology. My final project was setting up a robot to be able to map and localize within it’s surroundings. This was made so that the robot could be used to demo the software to potential clients showing the viability of the service.
