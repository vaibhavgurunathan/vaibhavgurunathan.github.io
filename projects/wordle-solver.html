Wordle Solver Bot

Python • Statistics • Probability • Algorithm Design

The Motivation

After failing my first Wordle ever (the answer was "ardor" - a word I'd never heard of), I decided to create a bot that would ensure I'd never lose again. This project combines statistics, probability theory, and algorithmic thinking to solve Wordle puzzles optimally.

How It Works

The solver uses a statistics-based approach that calculates the probability of letters appearing in certain positions. As the game progresses and more information is revealed through green, yellow, and grey letter feedback, the algorithm continuously updates probabilities and refines its guesses.

Technical Implementation

  • Probability Analysis: Statistical analysis of letter frequencies and positional probabilities
  • Dynamic Updating: Real-time probability adjustments based on game feedback
  • Optimal Word Selection: Choosing words that maximize information gain
  • Efficiency Optimization: Minimizing average guesses needed to solve puzzles

Performance

The Wordle solver successfully solved the challenging "ardor" puzzle in just 5 guesses, demonstrating its effectiveness at cracking even difficult Wordle puzzles through intelligent probabilistic reasoning.

Key Achievements

  • Solves Wordle puzzles with high efficiency
  • Adapts strategy based on real-time feedback
  • Demonstrates practical application of statistical methods
  • Provides foundation for more advanced word games solvers
